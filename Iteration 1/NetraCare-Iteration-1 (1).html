03

What you'll build

A website where patients, staff and doctors log in and manage appointments, backed by a FastAPI service that refuses any booking that breaks a clinic rule.

Patient

  • Log in with mobile number and name
  • See doctors and their specialities
  • See free slots, then book or cancel
  • See only their own appointments

Staff

  • See every appointment, by day and doctor
  • Book for any patient, including walk-ins
  • Cancel any appointment, even inside 2 hours

Doctor

  • Pick their name to log in
  • See their own schedule for a chosen day
  • See which patient is in each slot
  • Mark a visit as completed
04

Requirements

R1

Load the data pack

  • Create database tables for doctors, weekly schedule, holidays, patients and appointments.
  • A script or startup step loads the CSV files. Appointments start empty and are made through your app.

Check: after loading, you can log in as Asha Verma (9000000001) and see all 5 doctors.

R2

Completed visits

  • A doctor can mark an appointment as completed after the visit.
  • Follow-ups need this: a patient can only book a follow-up with the doctor who completed their last visit in that speciality.

Check: after Dr. Rao completes a Cataract visit, that patient can book a Cataract follow-up with Dr. Rao but not with Dr. Iyer.

R3

Simple login for three roles

  • No passwords. Patient enters mobile and name; a new mobile creates a new patient. Doctor picks their name from a list. Staff enters a name.
  • The backend, not only the screen, checks the role on every request. A patient asking for someone else's appointment is refused.

Check: one patient cannot see or cancel another patient's appointment, even by calling the API directly.

R4

Free slots

  • For a doctor, date and visit type, list the start times that can be booked.
  • Start times are on 10-minute marks. A new consultation takes 20 minutes, a follow-up 10.

Check: Dr. Rao offers no slots on Thursday morning (surgery), and a slot disappears once someone books it.

R5

Book and cancel

  • Every action goes through one rules check in the backend. A refusal returns a clear message that says which rule was broken.
  • A patient can cancel until 2 hours before the appointment. After that, the app says "Please contact Staff", and Staff can cancel it.
  • A cancelled slot becomes free for anyone to book.

Check: every row in the manual checks (section 09) behaves as described.

R6

Screens for each role

  • React or Next.js frontend that calls your API. Staff and Doctor views can be simple tables.
  • Show refusals in plain words, for example "Dr. Iyer is not at the clinic on Thursdays".

Check: you can do every action in section 03 from the browser.

05

Rules and safety in this iteration

This iteration enforces all of brief section 04 except doctor leave, which comes in iteration 4, and rescheduling, which is a stretch goal. The brief's targets start counting here: 0 bookings that break a clinic rule.

  • Put every rule in the backend, in one place. Never rely on the frontend to hide bad options. Later, an AI agent will call your API directly with no screen in between.
  • Patients only ever see their own appointments and details (brief section 07).
  • Use only the data pack. No real patient information, ever.

These logins are not real security. They are enough for a classroom project. A real clinic system would need proper authentication.
