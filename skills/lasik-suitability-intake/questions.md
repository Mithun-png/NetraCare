# LASIK pre-assessment questions

Ask these one at a time, in this order. Record each answer as the patient gives it.

1. How old are you?
2. Do you wear glasses, contact lenses, or both?
3. If you wear contact lenses: are they soft or rigid (RGP), and when did you last wear them?
4. Has your glasses or lens power changed in the last year?
5. Have you had any eye surgery, eye injury or eye infection before?
6. Do you often have dry, gritty or burning eyes?
7. Do you have diabetes, or any long-term health condition? Do you take any regular medicines?
8. Are you pregnant or breastfeeding?
9. What do you mainly want help with: distance vision, reading, sport, work, or something else?

## Intake notes format

Save the confirmed answers as `intake_notes` on the booking, one line per question:

```
LASIK intake (patient's own answers, not assessed)
1. Age: ...
2. Glasses/lenses: ...
3. Lens type and last worn: ...
4. Power change in last year: ...
5. Previous eye surgery/injury/infection: ...
6. Dry or gritty eyes: ...
7. Health conditions and medicines: ...
8. Pregnant or breastfeeding: ...
9. Main goal: ...
```
