# Children's visit

Clinic guidance for eye checks and appointments for children under 16 at NetraCare.

## Doctor and Speciality

- All children under 16 see our Paediatric eye specialist, **Dr. Vikram Menon** (Paediatric and Squint), whatever the eye complaint.
- Dr. Vikram Menon's OPD days (bookable):
  - Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday (morning session: 09:00–13:00, afternoon session: 14:00–18:00).
  - Wednesday and Friday: Not at the clinic (surgery days / off).

## Visit Guidelines and Preparation

- **Parent/Guardian Rule:** A parent or guardian must come with the child and stay for the entire visit. This is mandatory for all children under 16.
- **What to bring:**
  - The child's current glasses (if they wear them).
  - Any previous eye reports, prescriptions, or school eye-check reports.
  - A light snack and a favourite toy to keep the child comfortable.
- **Duration and Drops:**
  - Time at the clinic is about 1½ hours.
  - Dilating / focusing-relaxation drops take about 40 minutes to work.
  - After drops: the child's near vision is blurred and pupils remain dilated for up to a day. Bring sunglasses or a brimmed cap for the trip home outdoors.
- **Indicative Cost:**
  - ₹900 for a children's eye check (including the consultation).

## Steps for Assistant

1. Explain that children under 16 are seen by **Dr. Vikram Menon**, who is available on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday.
2. Share the mandatory guardian requirement, what to bring, clinic duration (about 1½ hours due to 40-minute drops), post-drops blurring (up to 24 hours), and the indicative cost (₹900).
3. Offer to find an available slot with Dr. Vikram Menon.
4. If the patient agrees, use `find_slots` for Paediatric with Dr. Vikram Menon and offer the earliest slots.

## Safety Rules

- NEVER name a diagnosis or likely medical condition (e.g. myopia, lazy eye, strabismus, amblyopia).
- NEVER prescribe or recommend eye drops or medications.
- If the query describes a red-flag symptom (sudden vision loss, injury, severe pain, chemical splash), stop immediately and provide the emergency response. (Code red-flag check handles this first.)
- Give the source as: Skill: children-visit.
