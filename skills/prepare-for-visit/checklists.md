# Visit checklists

Taken from clinic_policy_faq.md and procedures.md.

## Every visit

- Bring: your glasses and sunglasses, your contact lens case if you wear lenses, previous eye reports, prescriptions and discharge papers, and a list of all your medicines, including eye drops.
- Arrive 15 minutes before your appointment time.
- Your appointment time is when the doctor sees you. Tests and drops can make the visit longer.

## Dilating drops (General, Cataract, Retina, Paediatric)

- If your doctor uses dilating drops, they take 20 to 30 minutes to work, and your vision is blurred for 4 to 6 hours.
- Do not drive or ride a two-wheeler home. Bring someone with you, or use a taxi.
- Bring sunglasses for the journey home.
- Plan for about 1 to 1½ hours at the clinic.

## General

- Before: do not wear contact lenses on the day. Wear your glasses.

## Cataract

- Before: do not wear contact lenses on the day.
- Bring: your current glasses, even if they no longer help much.

## Glaucoma

- Before: keep using your eye drops as usual unless your doctor told you otherwise. Bring the drops with you.
- Before: get a good night's sleep if you are having a visual field test. It needs concentration.
- Afterwards: no special care, unless you were also given dilating drops.
- Plan for about 1 hour at the clinic if you are having tests.

## Retina

- Before: eat and take your diabetes medicines as usual.
- Bring: your latest sugar report (HbA1c), if you have one.
- Plan for about 1½ hours at the clinic.

## Dry eye

- Before: do not use any eye drops for 2 hours before the visit. Do not wear contact lenses on the day.
- Afterwards: a yellow dye may tint your tears for an hour.
- Plan for about 45 minutes at the clinic.

## Cornea and Refractive

- Before a LASIK suitability check: stop soft contact lenses 1 week before and rigid (RGP) lenses 3 weeks before.
- Before any other Cornea or Refractive visit: do not wear contact lenses on the day.

## Paediatric and Squint

- A parent or guardian must come and stay for the whole visit.
- Bring: the child's glasses and any school eye-check report. A snack and a favourite toy help.
- Plan for about 1½ hours, because the drops take 40 minutes to work.
- Afterwards: the child's near vision is blurred and the pupils stay large for up to a day. Sunglasses or a cap help outdoors.

## Children (any speciality, under 16)

- A parent or guardian must come and stay for the whole visit.
