# NetraCare clinic policy and FAQ

NetraCare Eye Clinic is a fictional clinic used for this course. This document answers common questions about timings, booking, visits and what the assistant can help with.

## Timings

- Open Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM.
- Closed for lunch from 1 PM to 2 PM.
- Closed on Sundays and on clinic holidays.
- Each day has two sessions: morning (AM) 9:00 to 13:00, and afternoon (PM) 14:00 to 18:00.

## Holidays

- The clinic is closed on its listed holidays, such as Diwali week and staff training days. No appointments can be booked on those dates.
- The booking page only shows dates when the clinic is open.

## Contact

- Front desk: 1800-000-0000 (fictional number for this course), Monday to Saturday during clinic hours.
- For anything the assistant cannot help with, please ask Staff at the front desk.

## Booking an appointment

- Book on the NetraCare website after logging in with your mobile number and name.
- Appointments can be booked up to 30 days ahead.
- You can have one active booking per speciality.
- A new consultation is 20 minutes. A follow-up is 10 minutes.
- A follow-up is booked with the doctor who saw you before.
- If no suitable slot is free in the next 30 days, the website says so. Please check again later, as cancelled slots become free for anyone.

## Consultation fees

- New consultation: ₹800.
- Follow-up consultation: ₹400.
- Tests and procedures are charged separately. See the procedures guide for indicative costs.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment

- You can cancel your own appointment on the website until 2 hours before it starts.
- Changing an appointment counts as cancelling it and booking a new one, so the same 2-hour limit applies.
- Within 2 hours of the appointment, please contact Staff.

## What to bring

- Your glasses and sunglasses, and your contact lens case if you wear lenses.
- Previous eye reports, prescriptions and discharge papers.
- A list of all the medicines you take, including eye drops.
- For a child, a parent or guardian must come.

## Arriving for your visit

- Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment time.
- Your appointment time is when the doctor sees you. Tests and dilating drops can make the full visit longer. See the procedures guide.

## Dilation

- Many eye checks use dilating drops that widen the pupil so the doctor can see the back of the eye.
- The drops take 20 to 30 minutes to work.
- Afterwards, vision is blurred and bright light feels uncomfortable for about 4 to 6 hours.
- Do not drive or ride a two-wheeler home after dilation. Bring someone with you, or use a taxi.
- Bring sunglasses for the journey home.

## Contact lenses

- Do not wear contact lenses on the day of an eye examination or a dry eye assessment. Wear your glasses and bring your lens case.
- Before a LASIK suitability check, stop soft lenses 1 week before and rigid (RGP) lenses 3 weeks before.

## Children

- Children under 16 are seen by the Paediatric doctor, Dr. Vikram Menon.
- A parent or guardian must stay with the child for the whole visit.

## Emergencies

- Some symptoms need urgent attention and must not wait for an appointment: sudden loss of vision, a curtain or shadow across the vision, a sudden shower of new floaters or flashes, a chemical splash or injury to the eye, and severe eye pain with headache, nausea or haloes around lights.
- If you have any of these, come to the clinic now or go to the nearest emergency department.

## What the assistant can and cannot do

- The assistant answers questions from NetraCare's documents and suggests which speciality to book.
- It cannot diagnose, say what condition you might have, suggest treatment or medicines, or read eye reports or images. Only a doctor can do that, at your visit.
- It cannot take payments or help with insurance claims. Please ask Staff at the front desk.
- It only ever discusses your own appointments and details.

## Privacy

- NetraCare uses your mobile number and name to identify you.
- Staff and your doctor can see your appointments and visit notes. Other patients cannot.
