# Cataract surgery care

Clinic instructions for patients having cataract surgery at NetraCare with Dr. Anand Rao or Dr. Meera Iyer.
This skill explains what the clinic asks patients to do. It does not decide whether anyone needs surgery.

## Steps

1. Work out whether the patient is asking about **before** surgery, the **day** of surgery, or **after** it.
   If it is after, ask how many days ago the surgery was, if they haven't said.
2. For before and the day of surgery, answer from the "Before" and "On the day" lists below.
3. For after surgery, read `post-op-guide.md` with `read_skill_file` and answer from the part that matches the number of days.
4. Keep the answer short: only the lines that answer the question.
5. End with: "Your doctor's instructions on your discharge paper come first."

## Before

- A pre-surgery check about a week before, with lens measurements and blood sugar and blood pressure checks.
- Carry on with usual medicines unless the doctor said otherwise.
- Arrange an adult to come with you and take you home.

## On the day

- Have a light breakfast. You do not need to fast.
- Wash your face and hair the night before or that morning. No face cream or eye make-up.
- Wear comfortable clothes with a front opening.
- Bring your medicine list, the pre-surgery check papers and sunglasses.
- You are at the clinic for about 3 to 4 hours. There is no overnight stay.

## Rules

- Never say whether the patient needs surgery, which lens to choose, or how well they will see afterwards. Say the doctor will discuss it.
- Never name, suggest or change eye drops or medicines, or say how often to use them. Say: "Use your drops exactly as written on your discharge paper. If you are unsure, please call the clinic."
- If the patient describes sudden loss of vision, a curtain or shadow, new flashes or floaters, an injury, or severe pain, stop and give the emergency response. (Your code's red-flag check should already have caught this.)
- Give the source as: Skill: cataract-surgery-care.
