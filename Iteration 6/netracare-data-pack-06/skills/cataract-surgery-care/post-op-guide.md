# After cataract surgery: day by day

NetraCare's instructions for the weeks after cataract surgery. The doctor's instructions on the discharge paper come first.

## Day of surgery (day 0)

- Go home and rest. Keep the eye shield on.
- Mild grittiness, watering and blurred vision are expected on the first day.
- Light activities such as walking around the house, watching TV and listening to music are fine.

## Day 1

- Come for the check at the clinic the next day. Bring your drops and the discharge paper.
- Start the drops exactly as written on the discharge paper.

## Week 1 (days 1 to 7)

- Wear the eye shield at night, every night, for 1 week.
- Wear dark glasses outdoors.
- Do not let soap or water into the eye. Wash your hair with your head tilted back, or ask someone to help. Clean the face around the eye with a clean damp cloth.
- No eye make-up.
- Reading, watching TV and using a phone are fine. Rest if the eye feels tired.
- Short walks are fine. Avoid dusty and smoky places.

## Weeks 1 to 2

- Do not lift heavy weights or bend low with your head below your waist for 2 weeks.
- Do not rub or press the eye.
- Light housework is fine.

## Weeks 2 to 4

- Do not swim for 4 weeks.
- Travel by car or train as a passenger is fine.

## Driving

- Do not drive until the doctor says your vision is safe for it. This is usually discussed at a follow-up check.

## Glasses

- Your glasses power usually changes after surgery. New glasses are checked at a follow-up, usually after about 4 weeks.

## Contact the clinic at once

Contact the clinic now, or go to the nearest emergency department, if you notice any of these after surgery:

- Vision suddenly gets much worse
- Pain that keeps getting worse
- Redness that keeps getting worse
- New flashes, a shower of floaters, or a curtain or shadow across the vision
